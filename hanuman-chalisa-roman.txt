Hanuman Chalisa

Doha
Shri Guru charan saroj raj,
nij manu mukuru sudhari.
Baranau Raghubar bimal jasu,
jo dayaku phal chari.
Buddhiheen tanu janike,
sumirau Pavan-kumar.
Bal buddhi bidya dehu mohi,
harahu kales bikar.
Chaupai
Jai Hanuman gyan gun sagar,
Jai Kapis tihun lok ujagar.
Ram doot atulit bal dhama,
Anjani-putra Pavan-sut nama.
Mahabir bikram Bajrangi,
kumati nivar sumati ke sangi.
Kanchan baran biraj subesa,
kanan kundal kunchit kesa.
Hath bajra au dhwaja birajai,
kandhe moonj janeu sajai.
Sankar suvan Kesari-nandan,
tej pratap maha jag bandan.
Bidyavan guni ati chatur,
Ram kaj karibe ko atur.
Prabhu charitra sunibe ko rasiya,
Ram Lakhan Sita man basiya.
Sookshma roop dhari Siyahin dikhava,
bikat roop dhari Lanka jarava.
Bhim roop dhari asur sanhare,
Ramchandra ke kaj sanvare.
Laye Sajivan Lakhan jiyaye,
Shri Raghubir harashi ur laye.
Raghupati kinhi bahut badai,
tum mam priya Bharat-hi sam bhai.
Sahas badan tumharo jas gavain,
as kahi Shripati kanth lagavain.
Sanakadik Brahmadi munisa,
Narad Sarad sahit Ahisa.
Jam Kuber digpal jahan te,
kabi kobid kahi sake kahan te.
Tum upkar Sugrivahin kinha,
Ram milaye raj pad dinha.
Tumharo mantra Bibhishan mana,
Lankeshwar bhaye sab jag jana.
Jug sahasra jojan par bhanu,
lilyo tahi madhur phal janu.
Prabhu mudrika meli mukh mahin,
jaladhi langhi gaye acharaj nahin.
Durgam kaj jagat ke jete,
sugam anugrah tumhare tete.
Ram duare tum rakhvare,
hot na agya binu paisare.
Sab sukh lahai tumhari sarna,
tum rachchhak kahu ko dar na.
Aapan tej samharo aapai,
teenon lok hank te kanpai.
Bhoot pisach nikat nahin aavai,
Mahabir jab naam sunavai.
Nasai rog harai sab pira,
japat nirantar Hanumat bira.
Sankat te Hanuman chhudavai,
man kram bachan dhyan jo lavai.
Sab par Ram tapasvi raja,
tin ke kaj sakal tum saja.
Aur manorath jo koi lavai,
soi amit jivan phal pavai.
Charon jug partap tumhara,
hai parsiddh jagat ujiyara.
Sadhu sant ke tum rakhvare,
asur nikandan Ram dulare.
Asht siddhi nau nidhi ke data,
as bar deen Janaki mata.
Ram rasayan tumhare pasa,
sada raho Raghupati ke dasa.
Tumhare bhajan Ram ko pavai,
janam janam ke dukh bisravai.
Ant kal Raghubar pur jai,
jahan janma Hari-bhakt kahai.
Aur devta chitt na dharai,
Hanumat sei sarb sukh karai.
Sankat katai mitai sab pira,
jo sumirai Hanumat balbira.
Jai Jai Jai Hanuman Gosain,
kripa karahu Guru Dev ki nain.
Jo sat bar path kar koi,
chhootahi bandi maha sukh hoi.
Jo yah padhai Hanuman Chalisa,
hoy siddhi sakhi Gaurisa.
Tulsidas sada Hari chera,
kijai Nath hriday manh dera.
Doha
Pavan-tanay sankat haran,
mangal moorati roop.
Ram Lakhan Sita sahit,
hriday basahu sur bhoop.
Aarti
Mangal moorati Marut-nandan,
sakal amangal mool nikandan.
Pavan-tanay santan hitkari,
hriday birajat Avadh Bihari.
Matu pita Guru Ganpati Sarad,
Shiv samet Shambhu Shuk Narad.
Charan kamal bindhau sab kahu,
dehu Ram-pad nehu nibahu.
Jai Jai Jai Hanuman Gosain,
kripa karahu Guru Dev ki nain.
Bandhan Ram Lakhan Vaidehi,
yah Tulsi ke param sanehi.

Siyavar Ramchandra ji ki jai.
